Kitchen Renovation Brisbane Comparison Explained

The first decision is scope rather than finishes. A makeover, a standard renovation and a custom project sit in different cost and timing bands, so the brief should state what stays, what moves and what has to be replaced before quotes are compared. A makeover typically takes two to four weeks and focuses on surface changes. A standard renovation spans four to eight weeks and may involve layout changes. A custom project takes eight to twelve weeks and involves bespoke joinery. Common triggers include outdated kitchens, lack of storage, inefficient layouts, and worn benchtops. If the problem is storage, note the pinch points. If the issue is layout, mark which appliance, sink or prep zones are frustrating now. The published range of about $15,000 to $60,000+ is best treated as a scoping signal, not a promise.

Timeline Expectations

Timelines vary by project type. Makeovers take two to four weeks. Standard renovations take four to eight weeks. Custom projects take eight to twelve weeks. The difference lies in demolition, service changes, and the complexity of custom joinery. If you need cooking access, coordinated appliance delivery or a clear completion window, ask which assumptions sit behind the proposed timeframe. Compliance and Licensing

Compliance checks are vital. In Queensland, a QBCC licence is required for building work over $3,300. Licensed plumbers and electricians are also required by law. The source page states that every specialist holds a current QBCC licence. This ensures proper insurance and accountability. Return on Investment

Kitchen renovations in metro Brisbane typically deliver a 60 to 80 per cent return on investment. Real estate agents consistently rank the kitchen as the most important room for resale value. Investing in durability, storage, and layout makes financial sense. This guide helps you plan the brief before asking for quotes, ensuring you get the best value for your investment.
